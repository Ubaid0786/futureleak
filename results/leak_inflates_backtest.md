# A full-sample z-score inflates a backtest with no edge

Generated by `experiments/leak_inflates_backtest.py`. Do not edit by hand.

- Python 3.14.8 on Windows, pandas 3.1.0, numpy 2.5.4, futureleak 0.1.0
- 1000 driftless geometric random walks per backtest length (seeds 0..999), daily volatility 0.01
- Strategy: short when the z-score of price is positive, long when negative, traded with a one-day lag
- Both versions are scored on the same days (after a 20-day warm-up); Sharpe ratios are annualised with 252 days
- leaky: z-score with the mean and standard deviation of the whole path; causal: expanding mean and standard deviation
